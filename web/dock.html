Tag outlines are burned in by the Jetson, in magenta, with the id and range. The cameras look port and starboard, so anything ahead of the bow is at the edge of both frames — that is where the tags will be, and it is normal. If the picture is the narrow 2:1 detector band rather than the whole fisheye, the tags are outside it: restart sender.py with --preview-full.
