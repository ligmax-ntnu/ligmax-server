Mark source — what the boat may steer on

With both lidars down the world model has no marks unless one of these is on: buoys then degrades to blind GNSS transit, quietly. Off is the vessel default, and it is the honest setting for a run nobody has watched the mask on yet.

Which side do the marks go?

This switch only changes the verdict shown on this page, never the boat. The course above is sent as buoyage: "route", so the vessel takes red to port and green to starboard on every leg — the direction of travel is the direction of buoyage, and the venue's 135° inversion does not apply to a ring. That is what the brute-force rule already does: a red seen to starboard shifts the aim point to starboard, a green seen to port shifts it to port. Leave this on red to port unless the jury says otherwise, and if they do, change the plan and not just this switch.

45 % down the frame

Nothing above that line is counted. The lidar's plane projects to a row in each frame and everything below it is the near water where the marks are; above it is shoreline, trees and the far side of the fjord, which are the things that put a red blob where no buoy is. Set it by eye against the horizon — it is a page control, not a vessel setting, and the vessel's own cut is colour_marks.py's.
