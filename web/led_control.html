Read-only session — every control below is disabled. Open this page with ?key=<admin key> to make changes.

Paint

Click or drag across LEDs to paint them the current colour. Groups shown as counted on the boat: 28 · 23 · 21 · 28 (100 LEDs total, wire order left to right - one LED in the third group was wired out after it failed).

Frames

One frame is a static colour or picture. Two or more, each with its own hold time, loop as an animation once sent. Preview plays locally in the browser and never touches the boat.

Saved effects

Saved on the ground station, not the boat - loading an effect replaces the frames above in this editor but changes nothing on the hull until Send below is pressed. Examples ship with the repo and are the same in every checkout: load one, edit it, save it under your own name. They cannot be overwritten or deleted from here - they are generated by tools/gen_light_effects.py.

Refresh rate

fps current: —

How often the hull worker redraws - the breathe and strobe as well as any pattern loaded above. 1-60, defaults to 30 and resets to that on a vessel restart. Independent of a frame's own hold time: this changes how smoothly the strip is repainted, not how long a frame is shown.

Headlight covers

Left ° boat: —
Right ° boat: —

The two servos that swing the headlight covers, straight to an angle each — the only control on this page that moves a mechanism rather than lighting one, so it goes nowhere near the pattern above and is not part of the standard/custom switch. Nothing here is gated by KILLED either: a cover is not a signal. Release the slider (or press a preset) to send; dragging alone changes nothing on the boat.
The two sides are mirrored — left opens by increasing the angle, right by decreasing it — so the same number is not the same place on the two covers, and each slider is labelled with its own ends. The travel shown is the span between the two hand-calibrated endpoints in lights_esp.ino, and it is the whole of what anyone has measured: past an endpoint the cover drives into its own stop, so an angle outside it is refused here, refused again on the vessel, and clamped by the firmware. Commanded, never measured — these servos have no feedback, so "boat:" is the angle the ESP32 was last told, not one it confirmed.

Send

Loads the pattern on the vessel but does not display it unless the switch above is set to "custom" - and never while the boat is KILLED, whatever the switch says. That guarantee lives on the vessel itself, in lights.py, not in this page.
